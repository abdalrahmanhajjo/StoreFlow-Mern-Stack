# Product

## Register

product

(The `/` landing page is a brand surface and is designed in the brand register; every in-app screen is product register.)

## Users

Independent retail operators — store owners, managers, and cashiers of supermarkets, pharmacies, restaurants, boutiques, convenience and electronics stores — plus a cross-tenant platform admin. Cashiers live in the POS all day; owners check dashboards and reports between tasks; the platform admin moderates tenants. Multi-locale (LTR + RTL), touch and keyboard.

## Product Purpose

StoreFlow is a multi-tenant retail SaaS: point of sale, catalog, inventory, suppliers and purchase orders, customer loyalty, reporting, and platform administration in one place. Success = a cashier rings a sale in seconds and an owner trusts the numbers.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, shopkeeper-serious. The visual identity is a committed monochrome editorial system chosen by the owner from a concrete reference: near-black ink on warm neutral paper, white panels with 1px hairlines, a constant dark shell (sidebar), tight grotesque typography (Archivo), uppercase micro-labels, and image-led product tiles. Photography of real goods is the color; the UI itself stays black and white.

## Anti-references

- The 2024 blue-gradient SaaS look (blue CTAs, soft shadows, glassmorphism) — this project was deliberately migrated away from it.
- Cream/beige "editorial serif" landing pages with italic display serifs and mono labels.
- Hero-metric templates (big number + small label + gradient accent) and identical icon-card grids.

## Design Principles

1. **The product is the imagery.** The landing page shows the real app (dark shell + POS) and real catalog photography, not abstract illustrations.
2. **Monochrome carries the brand; photographs carry the color.**
3. **Hairlines over shadows.** Structure comes from 1px borders and surface steps, not elevation.
4. **Data is real.** Marketing surfaces render live seed data (tenants, products, sales) — never lorem or fake round numbers.
5. **Light mode is canonical.** Dark mode mirrors it through tokens; the dark shell is constant in both.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Visible focus rings, ≥4.5:1 body contrast, reduced-motion alternatives for every animation, RTL-safe logical properties where locale demands, ≥16px inputs on mobile (iOS zoom), axe e2e checks in CI.
